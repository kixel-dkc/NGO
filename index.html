About Ubuntu NGO

Rooted in Ubuntu, building community through education

"Ubuntu" is a Nguni Bantu term meaning "humanity" or "I am because we are." Our NGO embodies this philosophy by building community through education, empowerment, and mutual support.

We aim to create a generation of empowered African youth who are proud of their heritage, equipped with essential life skills, and prepared to lead their communities toward a brighter future.

Our Core Programs

Two programs addressing real, everyday needs

We focus on the educational and practical needs of the children in our communities.

Pan-African Studies

African history and cultural education that helps children take pride in their heritage and understand their place in the world.

  • African history and civilizations
  • Cultural traditions and languages
  • African leaders and change-makers
  • Contemporary African issues
Enroll a child

Feminine Hygiene Support

Free sanitary products and menstrual health education so girls can attend school without interruption.

  • Monthly sanitary product distribution
  • Menstrual health education
  • Reusable pad workshops
  • Community support groups

Our Community

Change happens together

Everything we do is built with the community — parents, teachers, and local leaders — so progress lasts long after we leave.

From classroom lessons to club meetings and distribution days, Ubuntu NGO is a shared effort built on the belief that our humanity is tied to one another.

Clubs & Activities

Where children discover their talents

We cultivate diverse clubs that help children explore their interests, develop skills, and build confidence in a supportive environment.

Debate Club

Develop critical thinking and public speaking through structured debates.

Music Club

Explore traditional and contemporary African music, and form performing groups.

Journalism Club

Learn reporting, publish a school newspaper, and document community stories.

Agriculture Club

Learn sustainable farming techniques and grow school gardens.

Environmental Club

Conservation projects, recycling initiatives, and environmental education.

Drama Club

Express creativity through theater and perform plays for the community.

Scouts Club

Develop leadership, survival skills, and community service.

Book Club

Explore African literature and develop a love for reading.

Football Team

Build teamwork, discipline, and fitness through organized training.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What does Ubuntu NGO do?

We teach Pan-African studies to children, distribute feminine hygiene products, and run diverse clubs that build skills, confidence, and leadership.

Where do you operate?

Our work is based in Nsawam Mahama Krom, Ghana, with partners and team members in Kenya and the United States.

How are donations used?

Donations fund educational materials, feminine hygiene products, and the running of our clubs and mentorship programs.

Can I start a club at my school?

Yes. Reach out through our contact form and we will help you set up and support a new club.

How can I volunteer or partner?

Use the Volunteer or Partner buttons in the Get Involved section, or email us at Ubuntucadets@gmail.com.
